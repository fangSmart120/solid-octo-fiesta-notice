## About the project

Claw creates structured workout playlists tailored to your training session — warm-up, main workout, and cool-down phases — matched to your current energy level. Your energy changes day to day; your playlist should too.

- **Platform**: iOS (SwiftUI + MusicKit)
- **Backend**: private API service (Go)
- **Status**: closed testing (TestFlight)
