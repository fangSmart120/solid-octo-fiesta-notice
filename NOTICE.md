## AI playlist generation

### OpenAI

- Playlist planning and song selection are generated by large language models accessed via the **OpenAI API** (<https://openai.com>).
- User prompts describing workout sessions and music preferences may be sent to this provider to generate playlists. No health data is ever included (see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)).

### DeepSeek (alternate provider)

- The backend supports **DeepSeek** (<https://www.deepseek.com>) as an alternate LLM provider. Only one provider is active at a time.

## Song tempo & key data

### GetSongBPM

- BPM (tempo), musical key, and related song metadata are provided by **[GetSongBPM.com](https://getsongbpm.com)**.
- This data is used to match songs to workout intensity (e.g., tempo-synchronized running playlists).
